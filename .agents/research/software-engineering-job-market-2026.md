# Software-engineering job market: what is hot in 2026?

**Research date:** 25 July 2026  
**Scope:** Global synthesis, with U.S., EU/UK and India evidence where comparable data exist.

## Bottom line

The market is not a broad tech boom. Hiring in advanced economies remains weak relative to 2020, while demand is concentrating in senior, AI-fluent and infrastructure-heavy work. LinkedIn reported U.S. hiring in May 2026 was 4.8% below May 2025 and about 22% below February 2020; technology hiring was more than 30% below its pre-pandemic pace. Indeed nevertheless found U.S. software-development postings rose almost 15% from February 2025 to June 2026, from a low base, while overall postings fell 7%. Software postings were still 27.5% below February 2020. [LinkedIn, 15 June 2026](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/resources/linkedin-workforce-report-june-2026); [Indeed Hiring Lab, 8 July 2026](https://www.hiringlab.org/2026/07/08/ai-and-job-postings-from-destruction-to-creation/)

**Most hiring in absolute numbers:** broad software development—especially backend, full-stack and product engineering—rather than any narrow AI, Go or Rust title. U.S. BLS projects about **129,200 software developer/QA/tester openings per year** in 2024–34 and 267,700 net new software-developer jobs, versus 23,400 annual openings for data scientists and 16,000 for information-security analysts. Globally, the World Economic Forum (WEF) also places software and applications developers among the roles producing the largest net job growth to 2030. [BLS software developers, updated August 2025](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Computer-and-Information-Technology/Software-developers.htm); [BLS data scientists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm); [BLS information security analysts](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/information-security-analysts.htm); [WEF, 7 January 2025](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/)

**Fastest growth:** AI/ML and data roles, followed by cybersecurity. LinkedIn found AI-engineering postings were nearly 7% of U.S. technical postings in September 2025, up 63% year over year, and AI-engineering hiring was up more than 25%. That is exceptional momentum but also proves the volume distinction: roughly 93% of technical postings were not AI-engineering postings. BLS projects 2024–34 growth of 34% for data scientists, 29% for information-security analysts and 16% for software developers. [LinkedIn AI Labor Market Update, 5 September 2025](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/ai-labor-market-update-header-sept-2025.pdf); [BLS, 16 July 2026](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/artificial-intelligence-information-technology-and-employment-2024-34.htm)

**Best risk-adjusted career bet:** become a strong backend/distributed-systems engineer who can ship applied AI and operate it in production. That combines the largest occupation base with the fastest new demand: APIs and data systems, cloud/platform fundamentals, evaluation/observability/security, and one production language plus Python. “AI engineer” without software depth is a narrower and more volatile bet.

## Role-by-role evidence

### 1. Broad software, backend and full-stack: the volume leader

BLS counted 1.69 million U.S. software developers in 2024 and projects 1.96 million in 2034: 16% growth and 267,700 net new jobs. It explicitly attributes demand to AI, IoT, robotics, automation, security software, connected products and EVs. WEF ranks software and applications developers both among the fastest-growing roles by percentage and among the largest-growing roles by absolute count globally through 2030. [BLS software developers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Computer-and-Information-Technology/Software-developers.htm); [WEF jobs outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/)

Official occupation systems do not cleanly split backend, full-stack and mobile, so no trustworthy global dataset supports a precise ordering among those titles. The defensible conclusion is that the broad software family has the most openings; backend/full-stack capture much of that work because almost every AI, mobile, data or enterprise product still needs services, integrations, identity, storage and business logic.

The recovery is uneven by seniority. Indeed attributes **71% of the May 2025–May 2026 increase** in U.S. software postings to senior roles and 37% to titles mentioning AI (the categories overlap). LinkedIn found entry-level SWE did not join the late-2025 rebound, although its longer trend largely tracked overall SWE hiring; 55% of U.S. CS graduates in 2023 and 2024 started outside SWE. [Indeed, 8 July 2026](https://www.hiringlab.org/2026/07/08/ai-and-job-postings-from-destruction-to-creation/); [LinkedIn U.S. Software Engineer Talent Landscape, February 2026](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/us-software-engineer-talent-landscape-2026.pdf)

### 2. Applied AI versus core AI/ML

**Applied AI engineers** integrate models into products: retrieval, agents, tool use, evaluation, guardrails, latency/cost and product workflows. **Core ML/research engineers** train or optimize models and usually face a higher math, systems or credential bar. Current demand favors both, but applied work should have more addressable employers because adoption is spreading outside tech.

LinkedIn recorded more than 25% year-over-year growth in AI-engineering hiring in 2025 and 63% growth in its share of technical postings. AI talent was under 1% of U.S. LinkedIn members, indicating scarcity. WEF’s global employer survey ranks AI/ML specialists among the fastest-growing jobs through 2030, and half of employers planned to reorient their business around AI opportunities. [LinkedIn, September 2025](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/ai-labor-market-update-header-sept-2025.pdf); [WEF press release, 8 January 2025](https://www.weforum.org/press/2025/01/future-of-jobs-report-2025-78-million-new-job-opportunities-by-2030-but-urgent-upskilling-needed-to-prepare-workforces//)

The caveat is cyclicality and concentration: foundation-model startups grew headcount 107% year over year in LinkedIn’s September 2025 data, while other AI-startup categories grew more slowly. “Prompt engineer” alone is not a durable career category; AI literacy is diffusing into ordinary roles—U.S. postings requiring AI-literacy skills rose more than 70% year over year. [LinkedIn, September 2025](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/ai-labor-market-update-header-sept-2025.pdf)

### 3. Data engineering, MLOps and AI infrastructure

There is no harmonized official “MLOps” occupation count. The demand case comes from converging first-party signals. The Linux Foundation’s survey of 500+ global hiring/training leaders found organizations understaffed in AI/ML engineering and operations (68%), FinOps/cost optimization (61%), cloud computing (59%) and platform engineering (56%). LinkedIn reported AI Agents as the fastest-growing AI-engineering skill of 2025 and also rapid growth in LLMOps. [Linux Foundation, 23 June 2025](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-report-finds-organizations-embrace-upskilling-and-open-source-to-meet-ai-driven-job-demands); [LinkedIn EMEA-LATAM insights, February 2026](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/EMEA-LATAMMonthlyInsights-Feb2026.pdf)

BLS data-scientist growth (34%, 82,500 net new U.S. jobs in 2024–34) and WEF’s ranking of Big Data Specialists first among fast-growing global roles support continued demand for the data layer, but neither measure is a direct data-engineer count. [BLS data scientists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm); [WEF jobs outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/)

### 4. Platform, SRE, DevOps, cloud and FinOps

Titles are converging: enterprises may advertise the same underlying reliability, automation, Kubernetes, observability and developer-platform work as SRE, DevOps, cloud engineer or platform engineer. Google’s 2025 DORA study of nearly 5,000 technology professionals found 90% of organizations had adopted at least one platform and linked high-quality internal platforms with capturing value from AI. CNCF’s 2025 research estimated 15.6 million cloud-native developers; backend and DevOps professionals led adoption, and 77% of backend developers used at least one cloud-native technology. [Google Cloud DORA, 23 September 2025](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-the-2025-dora-report); [CNCF, 11 November 2025](https://www.cncf.io/announcements/2025/11/11/cncf-and-slashdata-survey-finds-cloud-native-ecosystem-surges-to-15-6m-developers/)

This is strong evidence for durable work, but adoption is not the same as vacancies. Treat “platform/SRE/cloud” as a valuable specialization inside the large software market, not as proven to exceed backend hiring.

### 5. Cybersecurity engineering

Cybersecurity has the clearest long-duration demand after broad software. BLS projects information-security analysts to grow 29% from 2024 to 2034, adding 52,100 jobs with about 16,000 openings annually. WEF places networks and cybersecurity among the three fastest-growing skills to 2030, and information-security analysts among the 15 fastest-growing jobs. Linux Foundation respondents reported a 65% cybersecurity/compliance staffing gap. [BLS information security](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/information-security-analysts.htm); [WEF, 2025](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/); [Linux Foundation, 2025](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-report-finds-organizations-embrace-upskilling-and-open-source-to-meet-ai-driven-job-demands)

The strongest engineering sub-lanes are application/product security, cloud security, identity, detection engineering, security automation and AI security. Entry-level cyber titles often still expect prior systems/network experience, so software or platform engineering is a practical feeder path.

### 6. Frontend and mobile

Frontend is not dead. BLS projects web developers and digital designers to grow 7% in 2024–34, with about 14,500 U.S. openings annually, driven by e-commerce and mobile usage. But LinkedIn’s 2026 SWE study found new hires emphasizing cloud platforms and fast-growing AI tools more than JavaScript, HTML and CSS, which were more prominent five years earlier. [BLS web developers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/web-developers.htm); [LinkedIn SWE landscape](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/us-software-engineer-talent-landscape-2026.pdf)

The resilient version of this career is product engineering: TypeScript plus accessibility, performance, design systems, analytics/experimentation and enough backend/mobile-native depth to own outcomes. Pure implementation from static designs is the more exposed segment.

### 7. Embedded, robotics and edge

WEF includes autonomous/EV specialists among the fastest-growing global roles and identifies robotics/autonomous systems as a growth driver for seven of its ten fastest-growing jobs. U.S. proxies are positive but smaller: electrical/electronics engineering is projected to grow 7% through 2034 with 17,500 annual openings; computer-hardware engineering 7% with 4,700 annual openings. BLS also includes robotic engineers within mechanical engineering. [WEF jobs outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/); [BLS electrical/electronics engineers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/electrical-and-electronics-engineers.htm); [BLS computer hardware engineers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/computer-hardware-engineers.htm); [BLS mechanical engineers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/architecture-and-engineering/mechanical-engineers.htm)

This is a durable but geographically clustered market around semiconductors, automotive, industrial automation, aerospace, defense and device companies. C/C++, Python, real-time systems, controls and hardware debugging matter more than trendy title wording.

## Languages are signals, not occupations

GitHub activity measures code and contributors—not vacancies. Its 2025 Octoverse found TypeScript became the most-used language by monthly contributors; Python remained dominant in AI projects. Nearly 80% of new repositories used Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Java, C++ or C#. Go was in the overall top ten; earlier 2024 data placed Go third and Rust seventh among fastest-growing languages. This supports ecosystem relevance, **not** the conclusion that Rust or Go has more jobs than Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, Python or C#. [GitHub Octoverse 2025, updated 28 February 2026](https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/octoverse-a-new-developer-joins-github-every-second-as-ai-leads-typescript-to-1/); [GitHub Octoverse 2024](https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/octoverse-2024/)

- **Python:** strongest cross-role leverage for AI, data, automation and backend.
- **TypeScript:** broadest modern web/full-stack signal; very high ecosystem activity.
- **Java/C#:** less fashionable but durable enterprise volume.
- **Go:** excellent for cloud, platform, networking and backend; usually a skill requirement inside those roles.
- **Rust:** excellent for security-sensitive systems, infrastructure, embedded and performance work; smaller vacancy pool and steeper entry path.
- **C/C++:** durable in inference runtimes, embedded, robotics, games, semiconductors and performance systems.

Choose a domain first, then its language. “Rust developer” and “Golang engineer” searches undercount roles whose actual titles are systems, backend, platform or infrastructure engineer.

## Geography

### Global and Europe

WEF’s 2025–30 survey of more than 1,000 employers across 55 economies ranks Big Data Specialists, AI/ML Specialists and Software/Application Developers among the fastest-growing roles, but its covered occupations represent only a subset of the global workforce and are employer expectations, not observed vacancies. [WEF methodology and outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/)

EU ICT employment reached 10.45 million in 2025, up 2.6% year over year and 59.4% since 2015. Cedefop’s July 2026 forecast projects EU ICT professionals and technicians to grow 2.5% annually through 2035, with large country variation from 1.3% in Germany and Italy to 7.8% in Romania. Europe therefore remains structurally positive but not uniformly “hot.” [Eurostat, 27 May 2026](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260527-2); [Cedefop, 21 July 2026](https://www.cedefop.europa.eu/cs/news/cedefop-updates-skills-forecast-new-data-guide-europes-skills-and-labour-market-policy-2035)

LinkedIn’s January 2026 comparison illustrates the divergence: postings requiring AI-engineering skills were up 37% year over year in the UK and 54% in India, but flat in France and Germany. [LinkedIn, February 2026](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/EMEA-LATAMMonthlyInsights-Feb2026.pdf)

### India

India is one of the strongest AI-demand markets in the available platform data. Indeed found 11.7% of Indian postings mentioned AI in September 2025, up from 8.2% a year earlier; 38.6% of data/analytics and 22.8% of software-development postings mentioned AI. India ranked second among Indeed markets, but Indeed explicitly warns that its Indian data skew toward tech. [Indeed India, 25 October 2025](https://www.hiringlab.org/au/blog/2025/10/25/september-2025-in-labour-market-update-continued-growth-in-ai/)

WEF’s India respondents expect Big Data Specialists, AI/ML Specialists and Security Management Specialists to be among the fastest-growing jobs through 2030. India also has unusually strong semiconductor and computing investment signals. [WEF India profile, 7 January 2025](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/5-region-economy-and-industry-insights/)

For an India-based candidate, the best combination is still broad engineering plus specialization: backend/data/platform foundations, applied AI, and evidence of production delivery. Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune and NCR offer different mixes; onsite/hybrid access materially widens the pool.

## Seniority and career recommendation

The market rewards demonstrated production ownership more than course completion. The shortage is not “people who have tried an LLM API”; it is people who can frame a useful problem, build the system, measure quality, manage data/security/cost, and keep it reliable.

For the strongest mix of volume, upside and durability:

1. Build backend fundamentals: APIs, SQL/data modeling, distributed systems, testing and one cloud.
2. Add applied AI: Python, model APIs/open models, retrieval, tool use, evaluation, observability, security and cost.
3. Learn the operating layer: containers, CI/CD, infrastructure basics and production debugging.
4. Pick one differentiator: data engineering/MLOps, platform/SRE, security, or domain expertise such as fintech, healthcare, industrial or developer tools.
5. Show two end-to-end systems with real constraints and measurements. A deployed, observable system is a stronger signal than several toy chatbots.

For a new graduate, optimize first for a role that builds engineering judgment—even if its title is backend, data, systems analyst, QA automation or cloud support—then move toward the target specialty. For an experienced engineer, the shortest path is usually to add AI/data/platform depth to the domain already understood, not reset to junior “AI engineer.”

## Method and uncertainty

- **Observed current demand:** LinkedIn hiring/member and posting data, and Indeed postings. These are timely but platform-specific, influenced by membership, duplicate ads, occupation classification and geography.
- **Long-run demand:** U.S. BLS 2024–34 projections, Cedefop 2035 forecasts and WEF 2025–30 employer expectations. BLS is U.S.-specific; Cedefop is European; WEF is global but survey-based and incomplete.
- **Skills/ecosystem:** GitHub, CNCF, DORA and Linux Foundation. These show technology use and employer-reported gaps, not direct job counts.
- **Role taxonomy:** official datasets do not separately count applied AI, MLOps, DevOps, SRE, backend, full-stack, Go or Rust roles consistently. Rankings for those categories triangulate multiple sources and are deliberately qualitative.
- **Duration ranges:** inferred from the overlap between current momentum and forecast horizons. They mean “likely above-market demand,” not guaranteed uninterrupted growth.
- **No salary ranking:** global compensation is too location-, level- and company-dependent for a defensible single comparison.

The evidence is strongest for three conclusions: broad software remains the absolute-volume leader; AI/data and cybersecurity have the fastest percentage growth; and production infrastructure plus domain depth is the safest bridge between them.
