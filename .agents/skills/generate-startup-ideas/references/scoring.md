# Finalist scoring

Score each criterion from 1 to 10 and explain the score with evidence or an explicit uncertainty.

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Pain | 18 | Is the problem costly, frustrating, or consequential? |
| Frequency | 10 | Does the workflow recur often enough to support retention? |
| Spend signal | 14 | Are people already spending money, labor, or risk on it? |
| Reachability | 14 | Can the founder identify and reach the first users? |
| Differentiation | 10 | Is there a narrow reason to choose this over substitutes? |
| Buildability | 12 | Can a useful wedge be built within the stated constraints? |
| Timing | 8 | Is there a current change or trigger making the idea more viable? |
| Founder fit | 9 | Does the founder have relevant skill, access, or motivation? |
| Evidence confidence | 5 | Are the supporting signals current, independent, and direct? |

Calculate the weighted score out of 100:

`weighted total = sum(score / 10 × weight)`

## Verdict bands

- **80–100: pursue validation** — strong enough for a focused real-world test, not automatic building.
- **65–79: promising but exposed** — validate the fatal assumption first.
- **50–64: weak evidence or fit** — refine the wedge or gather better evidence.
- **Below 50: archive for now** — do not force a positive verdict.

## Winner decision

The highest weighted total is not automatically the winner. Prefer an idea with reachable users and a falsifiable seven-day test over one with attractive theory but inaccessible demand.

Always show:

- strongest evidence;
- weakest assumption;
- disconfirming evidence;
- what would change the verdict.
